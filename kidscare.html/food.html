A BALANCED PLATE

WHY A HEALTHY PLATE MATTERS

Eating a balanced diet is essential for maintaining good health. A healthy plate consists of a variety of food groups, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. By incorporating these elements into your meals, you can ensure that your body receives the necessary nutrients to function optimally.
