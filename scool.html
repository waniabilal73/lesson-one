school subject discription

Mathematics
Mathematics is the study of numbers, shapes, and patterns. It helps develop problem-solving skills and logical thinking.
Science
Science is the study of the natural world through observation and experimentation. It helps us understand how things work and make discoveries.
English
English is the study of language and literature. It helps improve communication skills, reading comprehension, and writing abilities.
History
History is the study of past events and civilizations. It helps us learn from the past and understand how societies have evolved over time.
Physical Education
Physical Education focuses on physical fitness, sports, and overall health. It promotes an active lifestyle and teaches teamwork and discipline.
